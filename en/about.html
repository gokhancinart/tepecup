ABOUT US

Your trusted paper cup manufacturing partner.

Learn about Tepe Cup. Paper cup manufacturing in Istanbul, custom printed cups and nationwide delivery across Turkey.

Tepe Cup manufactures 4 OZ, 7 OZ and 8 OZ paper cups at our facility in Istanbul Ikitelli OSB. We serve coffee shops, restaurants, institutions and event organizers with printed and plain cup solutions.

Alongside our wide pattern catalog, we offer custom printing for your brand. With up to 4-color printing, you can carry your logo and corporate identity onto every cup.

We deliver nationwide and serve a broad reference portfolio — from municipalities and insurance companies to cafe chains and corporate brands.
