FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Frequently asked questions about paper cup orders, minimum quantities, printing options and delivery.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities vary by cup size and print type. Contact us via WhatsApp or phone for current details.

Do you produce printed and plain cups?

Yes. We offer ready-made patterned models as well as custom printed paper cups for your brand.

How many print colors are available?

We offer up to 4-color printing. Share your design file and we'll review technical compatibility together.

What cup sizes do you manufacture?

We offer 4 OZ, 7 OZ and 8 OZ paper cups. Each size suits different service needs.

What is the delivery time?

Stock products ship faster. For custom printed orders, timing depends on quantity and design approval.

Do you ship across Turkey?

Yes, we provide nationwide shipping across Turkey.
